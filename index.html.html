Our Story

A Rhode Island Tradition.

What started as neighborhood cookouts quickly turned into the city's worst-kept secret. Ocean State Hot Wieners was born out of our passion for cooking authentic local street food and honoring the great Rhode Island tradition.

We don't believe in shortcuts. We source our ingredients locally, and slow-cook our meat sauce using a closely guarded family recipe. It's a labor of love, and you can taste it in every bite.
